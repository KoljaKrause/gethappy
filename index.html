getHappy

Find wellbeing. Find your own path. Be present.

getHappy is a quiet companion for your everyday life. Choose the ten small things that make a good day for you, pause each evening to notice what you lived, and watch your days slowly fill with sunshine. No pressure, no noise. Just a gentle way to come back to yourself, one day at a time.

Ten small things

Your own definition of a good day. Simple moments that bring you closer to who you want to be.

Three moments of gratitude

Every day, write down three things you are grateful for. They gather in your personal journal, a quiet place to return to whenever you need it.

Ninety days of sunshine

A calm widget on your home screen shows how your days have been glowing. Not a scoreboard, a mirror.
